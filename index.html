About

Turning complex evidence into
better-informed decisions.

Health economics.
Disease simulation.
Cancer prevention.

I am a PhD researcher in health economics at the University of Oslo. My research focuses on lung cancer screening, using microsimulation and cost-effectiveness analysis to examine how screening strategies affect health outcomes and the use of healthcare resources.

My work combines computational modelling in C++ and R with health economic evaluation, including work with the Lung Cancer Policy Model (LCPM). My research experience also includes visiting research at the HMS-MGH Center for Health Technology Assessment.

Research

Simulating health outcomes.

Research focus

Lung cancer screening
in Norway

Studying how screening strategies can balance benefits, harms, and costs within a healthcare system. My work brings together population risk, disease simulation, and economic evaluation.

From evidence to decisions

  1. Population data
  2. Simulation
  3. Policy evaluation
  1. I.

    Screening strategies

    Evaluating approaches to lung cancer screening and their implications for population health and resource allocation.

  2. II.

    Microsimulation & disease modelling

    Using individual-level simulation, including LCPM, to connect risk factors, disease processes, and screening outcomes over time.

  3. III.

    Cost-effectiveness & uncertainty

    Comparing health outcomes and costs to support screening decisions, while examining uncertainty in model-based evidence.
